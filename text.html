Unlike <code>&lt;strong&gt;</code>, the <code>&lt;b&gt;</code> tag is purely for presentation and does not carry semantic meaning about importance. </div> <table> <thead> <tr> <th>Attribute</th> <th>Role</th> </tr> </thead> <tbody> <tr> <td>N/A</td> <td>The <code>&lt;b&gt;</code> tag has no specific attributes beyond global attributes</td> </tr> </tbody> </table> <div class="example-container"> <div class="example-code"> <pre><code>&lt;p>This text is &lt;b>bold&lt;/b> for styling.&lt;/p></code></pre> </div> <div class="example-result"> <h4>Rendered Result:</h4> <p>This text is <b>bold</b> for styling.</p> </div> </div> <!-- Italic Tag --> <h3>&lt;i&gt; Tag</h3> <div class="definition"> The <code>&lt;i&gt;</code> tag is used to apply italic styling to text.
